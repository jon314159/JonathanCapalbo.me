Self-directed portfolio case study · Hypothetical

A practical business analysis for a better arcade ticketing process.

For this case study, I planned how one arcade location could handle five common types of internal tickets in one place. The process covers broken machines, game-card issues, refunds, prize-counter problems, and facility incidents from the first report through final review and reporting.

Hypothetical and simulated The business, stakeholders, data, requirements, tests, and targets are fictional. ServiceNow is a conceptual platform frame only; no software was configured, built, tested, deployed, or measured.

Problem and recommendation

Bring scattered issue reports into one clear ticketing process.

In the simulated current state, staff report issues through conversations, paper notes, phone calls, and messages. With information spread across several channels, it is hard to see who owns the work, what is urgent, and whether an issue was fully resolved.

The recommended approach is one ticketing process for all five issue types at one location. This keeps the pilot focused while still testing shared routing, approvals, training, and reporting.

Proposed future state

A visible path from first report to verified closure.

  1. Minimum ticketCapture category, location, description, and urgency.
  2. TriageConfirm impact, priority, and safety or refund controls.
  3. Assign ownerRoute to the responsible operational team.
  4. Work or approveInvestigate, repair, fulfill, or obtain authorization.
  5. Place pendingRecord the reason when parts, vendor, or customer input blocks work.
  6. ResolveDocument the action taken and resolution code.
  7. VerifyConfirm service restoration or customer-facing completion.
  8. Close and reportLock the record into auditable history and KPI reporting.

Analyst workstream

Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. Frame the scopeDefine the problem, pilot boundary, assumptions, stakeholders, and decision rights.
  2. Model the workCompare the simulated current state with a proposed future-state flow and exception paths.
  3. Define controlsSet routing, priority, approval, status, security, and closure rules.
  4. Baseline requirementsDocument 110 draft requirements, 111 fields, and 20 cross-field validations.
  5. Trace and plan testsLink 34 stories and 102 acceptance criteria through 31 planned UAT cases.
  6. Prepare adoptionSpecify rollout, governance, an employee procedure, 16 KPIs, and three reports.
