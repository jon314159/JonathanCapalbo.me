Workplace initiative · Fictional process illustration

Making daily reports easier for teammates to find.

At Comcast/Xfinity, teammates needed information from multiple daily reports. Finding the right report and applying filters caused confusion and repeated manual work. I built Power Automate flows to filter and distribute reports through email and Microsoft Teams for a team of 10.

Confirmed workplace experience

Move useful information closer to the people using it.

The reporting problem

My team gathered daily metrics, including sales counts and team-adherence comparisons, from multiple reports. Teammates requested a more convenient way to access the information.

My contribution

I built flows that filtered the relevant reports and distributed them by email or in a Teams chat, depending on the need. I also used scheduled messages and confirmation emails in recurring workflows.
