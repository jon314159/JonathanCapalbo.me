Self-directed portfolio case study · Hypothetical

A ticketing plan for five types of arcade issues.

For this case study, I planned how one arcade location could handle five common types of internal tickets in one place. The process covers broken machines, game-card issues, refunds, prize-counter problems, and facility incidents from the first report through final review and reporting.

Hypothetical and simulated The business and planning scenario are fictional. This is a process proposal, with no configured software, executed UAT, or measured results.

Problem and recommendation

Bring scattered issue reports into one clear ticketing process.

In the simulated current state, staff report issues through conversations, paper notes, phone calls, and messages. With information spread across several channels, it is hard to see who owns the work, what is urgent, and whether an issue was fully resolved.

The proposed pilot covers one location and five issue types, with shared routing, approvals, training, and reporting. Limiting the location keeps the operating boundary clear; including five issue types still introduces different approval and exception paths.

Proposed future state

A visible path from first report to verified closure.

  3. Define controlsSet routing, priority, approval, status, security, and closure rules.

  6. Prepare adoptionPlan rollout, employee instructions, governance, and KPI definitions.

Process choices

What changes in the proposed workflow.

01

A shared record

Replace scattered conversations, notes, calls, and messages with a ticket containing category, location, description, and urgency. Staff would have a common place to check the issue.

02

Explicit ownership

Triage assigns a responsible team and identifies approval needs. Refunds and repairs follow different work paths within the same lifecycle.

03

A reason for waiting

A pending state records whether parts, vendor input, or customer information is blocking work. A ticket can remain open without making its next dependency invisible.

04

Verification before closure

Record the resolution, then verify service restoration or customer-facing completion before closing. This adds a check between doing the work and reporting it complete.
