What I built and checked

My teammates wanted an easier way to look up sales counts and compare team adherence. I set up the filters and delivery channels, and used scheduled messages and confirmation emails for recurring workflows.

I checked the screenshots posted in Teams against the expected date and filters. Reports arrived more consistently, and teammates spent less time finding and filtering them. I didn’t measure the time saved.
