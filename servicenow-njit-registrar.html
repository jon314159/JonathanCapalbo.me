Academic project · ServiceNow school lab

A ServiceNow portal designed to make registrar information easier to find.

For an NJIT school project, I built a searchable portal with 42 registrar articles organized into 12 categories at project completion. I also restructured the registration guide to support faster scanning of steps, common errors, and contact information.

The need

Design a clearer path to routine registrar information.

Registration, transcripts, graduation, privacy, and academic policies all involve different steps and deadlines. I organized the project around two navigation paths: search for an article or browse by topic.

I used clear category names, consistent article layouts, visible contact information, and ServiceNow version history to distinguish the published project version from the version marked outdated.

Project frame

Responsibilities, design decisions, and evidence limits.

My role

Configured the school-lab portal, organized the article library, and revised the registration article through version 3.0.

Design constraints

Focused on portal navigation and knowledge content. Request forms and ServiceNow catalog items were outside the project scope.

Example revision

Restructure a dense registration guide for step-by-step scanning.

In the project, I revised the registration article through version 3.0. The recreated excerpt starts with a short overview, separates preparation from the registration steps, explains common errors, and uses tables for holds and deadlines.

  • Short overview before detailed instructions
  • Numbered registration steps in task order
  • Separate explanations for common Banner errors
  • Scannable tables for holds and deadlines
