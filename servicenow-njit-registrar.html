NJIT coursework · ServiceNow

A registrar knowledge base

For an NJIT course, I built a ServiceNow portal for common registrar questions. I organized 42 articles into 12 categories and revised the Banner registration guide so students could find what to do when registration stalled.

See the registration guide revision Portal project notes

Design decisions

How would a student find the right article?

A student who knows the term “transcript” can type it into search. Someone trying to figure out what to do before graduation can browse Graduation & Degrees. I gave both routes a place on the home page.

Inside an article, I put preparation before instructions and kept the office contact details close to the steps where someone might need help.

Example revision

What to check when Banner blocks registration

I revised the guide through version 3.0. A student getting ready to register needed a checklist. A student already looking at a Banner error needed a way to look up that message and find the next step.

  • Short overview before detailed instructions
  • Numbered registration steps in task order
  • Separate explanations for common Banner errors
  • Scannable tables for holds and deadlines
  • Clear escalation and contact information
