The need

Design a clearer path to routine registrar information.

Registration, transcripts, graduation, privacy, and academic policies all involve different steps and deadlines. I organized the project around two navigation paths: search for an article or browse by topic.

I used clear category names, consistent article layouts, visible contact information, and ServiceNow version history to distinguish the published project version from the version marked outdated. The intended benefit was easier scanning for the relevant step or answer; no usability improvement was measured.
