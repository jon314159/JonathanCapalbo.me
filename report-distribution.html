What I built and checked

My teammates wanted to check sales counts and compare team adherence without searching each report. I set up the filters and delivery channels, scheduled recurring messages, and used confirmation emails.

When a report appeared in Teams, I checked its screenshot for the right date and filters. Reports arrived more consistently, and teammates spent less time looking for their figures. I didn’t track the time saved.
